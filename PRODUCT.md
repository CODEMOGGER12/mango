# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Vite, Three.js via React Three Fiber and drei, Motion for UI animation, Lenis for smooth scroll. Chosen by the user in the brief ("preferred stack"). No backend.

## Users

General online shoppers who find MANGO on the web: people who want a colorful, characterful hook for their clothes, by the door or the mirror, and buy on impulse because it looks good on a wall. Confirmed by the user.

## Product Purpose

MANGO sells one product: a handmade-style clay clothes hook that sticks to a wall. A rounded clay base goes on the wall; a contrasting sculpted clay piece pressed on top is the hook clothes hang from. (Confirmed by the user after the first build: "it's a cloth hanger u stick on a wall".) It comes in ten two-color variations. The site lets a visitor turn the object in 3D, pick a color, and put pieces in a cart. Success: a visitor understands the object, finds a color they like, and places an order request.

## Positioning

An independent creative studio, not a toy store. The object is deliberately imperfect: soft, slightly irregular, matte, tactile. The pitch is the feeling of the thing, not specs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ten variations, every one priced exactly ¥12 (RMB). No per-color pricing, no bundles, no discounts.
- Variation names and color pairs come from the brief: Vanilla (cream / charcoal), Matcha (sage / cream), Strawberry (soft pink / red), Ocean (blue / pale aqua), Lavender (purple / lilac), Sunset (orange / coral), Peach (peach / yellow), Midnight (navy / white), Lemon (yellow / orange), Cocoa (brown / beige).
- No online payment. The cart ends in an "order request": a summary and request number, with no payment taken. Confirmed by the user.
- The 3D product must be built in code; no photographs, downloaded models, or pre-rendered images of it.
- English-only copy. Confirmed by the user.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: MANGO, used as a typographic wordmark. No literal mango fruit imagery.
- Tagline offered by the brief: "Play takes shape."
- Personality: creative, playful, modern, tactile, minimalist, youthful, premium, experimental.
- Design reference pinned by the user: the dialedcoffee.com scrolling experience (screen recording supplied), to be translated into an original MANGO identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- One product photo supplied by the user (two pieces: white on charcoal, yellow on orange). Used only as a modelling reference.
- No testimonials, customers, reviews, dimensions, materials, certifications, or shipping terms exist. Never invent them.
- How the hook attaches to the wall and how much weight it holds are unknown. Say only that it sticks to a wall and holds clothes.

## Product Principles

1. The object leads. Every section either shows it or makes you want to hold it.
2. Imperfect on purpose. Irregular, handmade, matte; never glossy or machined.
3. One price, ten moods. Choosing a color is the whole decision; keep it effortless.
4. Say less. Short, human copy; no marketing superlatives.
